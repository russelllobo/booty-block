## Description

Bootyblock helps you put a little movement between you and your most distracting apps.

Choose the apps and categories you want to block with Apple’s Screen Time controls. When you want a scroll break, pick how many minutes you want to unlock and complete the matching number of squats.

Bootyblock uses your iPhone camera to count full-body squat repetitions. Camera frames are processed on your device and are not recorded or uploaded.

Features:

- Choose apps and categories with Apple’s native picker
- Block selected apps until you earn an unlock session
- Count squats with on-device pose detection
- Choose unlock sessions from 1 to 60 minutes
- Automatically restore blocking when earned time ends
- Recalibrate the camera whenever needed

Bootyblock requires Screen Time and Camera access to provide its core features.
